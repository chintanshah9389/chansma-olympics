# Indoor players by sport combination

Each player is one mobile number taken from player 1 or player 2 on an indoor sport. The contact number on the entry is not used. If the same player mobile appears on more than one sport, those sports are joined. A doubles partner is their own player. Turf cricket and overarm cricket are not included.

Gender is the category on the sport entry where that player mobile appears. A player mobile that appears in both a male entry and a female entry is marked Male and female.
